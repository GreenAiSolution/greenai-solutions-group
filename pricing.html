Asked before signing.

Do I need a contract?

No. Both services are month to month and you can cancel at the end of any month you have paid for. You keep every script, transcript and file.

Can I start with one and add the other later?

Yes. Start with whichever is leaking the most, add the other in any month. There is no bundle to buy into and no minimum.

Is there a setup fee or a per-lead charge?

No. $397 a month for the AI agent and $149 a month for review automation is the whole price. Anything you pay Google, Jobber, Housecall Pro or your text provider is your own subscription, at their price.

How long does setup take?

Usually inside two weeks from the day you answer the setup questions. You approve every reply and every review text before it goes live, and you get the timeline in writing with the price.

How do I pay?

Card through Stripe, with your details entered on Stripe's own page and never on this site, or an invoice if you prefer. First payment on order, then the same day each month.
