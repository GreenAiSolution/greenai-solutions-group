What It Actually Is

Three things get built. Then they get tuned every month.

One — five-minute lead response. Every new enquiry gets a real answer inside five minutes, 24/7, in your voice. Not a canned "thanks, we'll be in touch" autoresponder — a reply that moves the conversation forward. In almost every business we look at, this is the single biggest leak and the cheapest one to plug.

Two — a CRM that is actually used. Built or rebuilt so every lead is captured, scored and followed up automatically. Most businesses have either no CRM or one nobody opens. Both cost you the same amount of money.

Three — a prioritised roadmap. Where AI creates real leverage in your business, ranked, with the reason. Including the places it does not. A roadmap that recommends automating everything is a sales document, not a plan.

On top of that: email and SMS sequences, workflow automation across the repetitive work, a bi-weekly call with Jaden, and a monthly review of what the numbers actually did.

Why this one is "most popular"

Because it is usually the one that pays for itself first. Response time and follow-up are where money is already leaking — you do not need new leads to benefit, you need to stop losing the ones you have.

It is also the service that tells you honestly whether you need the other three yet.

Straight Answers

What we will tell you that a salesperson would not.

This service is worth what it costs only in specific conditions. Here are the ones where it is not.

  • ✕If you have almost no lead flow, fix that first. Response time on three enquiries a month is not where your money is. We will point you at ads or web instead, and say so plainly.
  • ✕If your offer is not working, automation makes it fail faster. Speeding up a route to a "no" gets you more nos, sooner.
  • ✕We do not promise a revenue number. Anyone quoting you one has not seen your books. The roadmap comes with reasoning, not a guarantee.
  • ✕This is not done-for-you sales. We build the system that puts a qualified lead in front of you. Closing is still yours — unless you also hire an AI employee to do the calling.

Questions

The things people ask before they buy.

What happens in the first thirty days?

The audit and roadmap land in the first two weeks, and five-minute lead response is usually live before the month is out. You will have something working, not just a document, by day 30.

Do I need a CRM already?

No. If you have one we rebuild it properly; if you do not, we pick one that fits your size and set it up. You are not forced onto anything expensive.

Is this just setting up some Zapier automations?

No. The automations are the last step. The value is in the audit that finds where your money is actually leaking and the ranking that says what to fix first — anyone can wire a trigger.

What if the roadmap says I should not buy anything else?

Then that is what it says. We would rather keep one honest client for three years than sell four services to someone who needed one.

Who am I actually talking to?

Jaden. Every call, every month. No account managers and no junior staff — that is the whole point of a five-client cap on the partnership tier and direct access on every other plan.

Can I cancel?

Any time, no notice. Everything built during the engagement stays yours and keeps running.
