Questions & Answers

The questions you would ask me anyway.

Everything asked before handing a phone to an AI. Answered straight, including the ones where the answer is no.

How it works

How it works (6)

What is an “AI employee”, in plain English?+

Software that picks up your phone, answers your texts and replies to your web-form leads, in your business’s own words. Not a chat bubble, and not a person. It answers in about eight seconds and puts the appointment straight into your calendar.

Do my customers know they are talking to an AI?+

If they ask, yes. It does not pretend to be a person and will not take a human name. Try it on the live demo.

Do I keep my own phone number?+

Yes. Your number stays yours: nothing on your truck or your Google listing changes. At setup you choose: AI answers first, or only the calls you miss.

What happens when it does not know the answer?+

It says so. Built to say “I’m not sure” rather than invent an answer. An invented answer is the one thing you’d have to apologise for. It takes the details and the conversation lands in your inbox.

What actually happens at 2am?+

The same thing that happens at 2pm. Answered in about eight seconds, nights, weekends, holidays. You read the transcript over coffee instead of losing the lead.

Does this replace my receptionist?+

No, and it is not sold as one. It covers what nobody is covering: the 7pm ring-out, the callback you meant to make Tuesday, the second caller while your person is on the line.

What it costs

What it costs (6)

What does the monthly price cover?+

One AI employee, unlimited leads, from $297 a month depending on the role. Answering every call, text and form; booking into your calendar; texting back missed calls and following up on day 1, 3 and 7; full transcripts plus a Monday summary. And me maintaining it, including changing what it says whenever you want. A busy month costs the same as a quiet one.

Is there a setup fee or a per-call charge?+

No to both. No setup fee, no per-call or per-minute charge. The monthly price is the whole price. Metered plans charge you more for having a good month.

What is not included?+

Anything paid to someone else, never marked up: a new phone number, any CRM it writes into, ad spend if you run ads with us. You hear about it before it is incurred.

How do I pay, and when am I charged?+

Card or bank debit through Stripe. Your details go on Stripe’s own checkout page, never on this site. First payment on order, then the same day each month. Prefer an invoice? Say so.

Can I cancel?+

Any time. No minimum term, no lock-in, no cancellation fee, email jaden@greenaidigital.com or call (480) 798-0753. It stops at the end of the month you already paid for: you cancel forward, not backward.

Do you guarantee I will get more bookings?+

No, and be wary of anyone who does. How many leads become jobs depends on your market, pricing and follow-through. What I stand behind is the system doing what I said it does.

Getting it running

Getting it running (4)

How long until it is live?+

About two weeks from the day you answer the setup questions: you tell us how you answer, we build it, you test it, it goes on duty.

What do you need from me?+

One round of questions (trade, hours, service area, prices, your usual questions), plus access to whatever it books into. If we end up waiting on you, go-live moves.

Can I hear it before my customers do?+

Yes, and you should try to break it. Nothing goes live until you have called your own number and tried to trip it up. The demo on this site is a working call in your browser right now.

What if I want it to say something differently?+

You tell me and I change it. No change fee, no ticket queue, no waiting for a release.

Risk, control and who you are dealing with

Risk, control and who you are dealing with (6)

What if it books a job I cannot do?+

You set the boundaries and it works inside them: your open slots, your services, your service area. Anyone outside that becomes a lead handed to you.

Who is responsible if it says something wrong?+

Straight answer: AI output can be wrong. That is why a second system checks the first, why it says “I’m not sure” instead of guessing, and why you approve what it says before it goes live. Tell me and it changes that day.

What happens to my customers’ information?+

It stays yours: your data, your configuration, your transcripts. If you leave, you take all of it with you.

I already use Jobber or Housecall Pro. Does this replace it?+

No, it feeds it. Bookings land in your calendar. Jobber, Housecall Pro or ServiceTitan: tell me at setup and we work out how bookings get there.

Do you only work with Arizona businesses?+

I am in Gilbert, Arizona, and a lot of the companies I build for are nearby. Nothing about it is physically local. Outside the Valley, email me and say so.

Who am I actually dealing with?+

Me: the founder of GreenAI Digital, in Gilbert, Arizona. I build it, I maintain it, and you deal with me directly rather than a rep or a support queue. (480) 798-0753.
