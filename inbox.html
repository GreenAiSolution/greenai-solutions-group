The job

What INBOX does inside Google Workspace

Six things, all of them in the tool you already open every day.

Replies to every new lead in under a minute

From the replies you approved, with the customer's details filled in and your questions asked.

Books into Google Calendar

Offers the slots you allow, sends the invite, adds the address and notes.

Follows up until they answer

Day one, three and seven. Stops the moment they say stop.

Files what matters to Drive

Quotes, photos and signed forms land in the folder you chose, named the way you name things.

Labels and summarises the rest

Every morning: what needs you, what it handled, what it was not sure about.

Drafts, never sends, anything unusual

A complaint, a refund, a legal question: drafted and waiting for you.

How it connects

Your account. Your rules.

  • Signs in as its own user in your Workspace

    You create a user for it, or give it delegated access to the inbox you choose. Every message it sends is in Sent under that name.

  • Google's own permissions

    Gmail, Calendar and Drive scopes only, listed in writing. Revoke them in your admin console any time.

  • You read every template first

    Nothing goes to a customer until you have read the wording.

Questions

Asked before hiring INBOX

All the questions
  • Does it send from my address?

    From the inbox and name you choose. Customers see your company, and you see everything in Sent.

  • What if it gets an email it does not understand?

    It drafts nothing to the customer and puts the thread in your "needs you" summary.

  • Will it spam my customers?

    Three follow-ups over a week, then it stops. Anyone who says stop is never contacted again.
