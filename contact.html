Before you write

Six things owners ask first.

Do I have to set anything up?+

We build it, connect it to your supported tools, and test it with you before launch. Your side of it is reading the scripts and saying yes. After that, every conversation shows up in your inbox - nothing new to log into.

How quickly does it go live?+

About two weeks from the day you answer the questions - the build takes your price list, your service area and your house rules, and you approve every script before a customer sees one. From the first day live, every call and message it handled lands in your inbox, so you can read exactly what it said and what it booked.

Will the AI sound like me?+

It sounds like your company, because you write the ceiling on it. You read every reply it can send before it goes live and change any wording you do not like - same day, at no charge, as often as you want. It also never claims to be a person.

What CRMs and tools do you integrate with?+

Tell us what you already use - your CRM, calendar, inbox, phone system or booking tool - and we will say plainly what can be connected before you buy.

Is there a long-term contract?+

No. Month to month, no minimum term and no cancellation fee. Call or email and it stops at the end of the month you have paid for - and you keep every script, transcript and configuration.

I am outside Arizona. Can you still do it?+

Yes. Everything is built and run remotely, so the city does not matter. Tell us where you are and what you do and we will say plainly whether it fits before you pay anything.
