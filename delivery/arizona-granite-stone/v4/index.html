Pick your stone

Granite, quartz, marble, and much more.

01

Granite

Born in heat. Hard to scratch, hard to scorch, and every slab is one of a kind.

It asks for a coat of sealer every year or two, a service we offer.

02

Quartz

Ground stone pressed with resin into a slab. Never needs sealing, never stains from coffee or wine.

It asks you to keep hot pans off it.

03

Marble

The softest, and the most beautiful. Veins no factory can print.

It asks for sealer, and for lemon and wine to be wiped up fast.

How a job goes

From phone call to finished kitchen.

  1. 1Call or send the form

    An exact pattern of your counters, sink and cooktop cut-outs.

  4. 4We cut it in our shop

  5. 5We install & seal

    Set, seamed and sealed where the stone needs it. Old tops hauled out if you ask.
