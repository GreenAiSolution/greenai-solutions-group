Straight talk

What the crew will and will not do.

What the crew does

  • Answers every call, reply and message, and introduces itself as AI
  • Books jobs only inside the prices, services and rules you approve
  • Records every call so you can check the work anytime
  • Hands anything tricky to you instead of guessing

What it will not do

  • Pretend to be a human. Callers always know it is AI.
  • Quote prices or promise timelines you did not approve.
  • Handle emergencies. Callers are told to hang up and dial 911.
  • Replace your judgment on big, unusual or sensitive jobs. Those go to you.
