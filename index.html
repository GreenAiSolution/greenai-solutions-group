Straight answers

Before you ask.

Which service should I start with?

If you need calls this month, start with ads plus automation. If you can wait, SEO is the cheapest long-term source of calls. The free growth check tells you which one fits your business.

How long does SEO take?

Weeks to months. Fixing your Google profile shows up fastest. Map rankings and city pages build over two to four months, and then they keep bringing calls without more ad spend. Nobody can honestly guarantee a ranking.

How much does SEO cost?

Local Starter is $1,097 a month for one city. Market Leader is $1,797 for up to five cities, and Valley Dominator is $2,997 for the whole Phoenix metro. No setup fee, month to month.

How does ad management pricing work?

You pay Google and Meta directly for the ads, on your own card. We charge a flat monthly fee: $897 for one platform, $1,497 for Google plus Facebook/Instagram, or $2,497 to scale, plus a one-time $797 launch. Premium AI ad creative is included.

Why do I need automation if I already run ads?

Because most leads go to whoever answers first. A lead that comes in at 9 PM and waits until morning has usually booked someone else. Automation texts them back in under a minute, follows up missed calls, and asks happy customers for reviews.

How much does automation cost?

One automation is $697 to build and $347 a month. Front Office, three working together, is $1,497 and $697 a month. Autopilot, up to eight, is $2,997 and $1,197 a month. The build price is fixed in writing before any work starts.

Is there a discount for all three?

Yes. The Full System (SEO Market Leader, Ads Growth and Automation Front Office) is $3,297 a month instead of $3,991, with a $997 setup instead of $2,294.

Why do you cost more than some agencies?

Most local agencies charge around $1,000 a month for SEO and $500 to $1,500 to run ads. We sit just above that because premium AI ad creative is included, you talk to the people doing the work, you own every account, and there is no long contract.

Do I have to switch software?

No. We work with your Google profile, your website, your phone and the apps you already use. If something cannot connect, we tell you before you pay.

Will the texts sound like a robot?

Messages are written in your voice and you approve them before anything goes live. Anything unusual gets passed to you instead of guessed at.

Do I own my accounts?

Yes. Your Google Business Profile, ad accounts and website stay in your name. If you ever leave, everything stays with you.
