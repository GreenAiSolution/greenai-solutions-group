The agreement

Here are the terms that matter most, in plain English. This is a summary — read the full Service Agreement (v1.1), which opens in a new tab and is what you are actually agreeing to.

You can leave whenever you want

Monthly plans are month-to-month. No minimum term, no lock-in, no cancellation fee. Cancel by email or phone and it stops at the end of the month you already paid for.

You own what we build

Your site, your ad creative, your copy, your configurations, your data — yours once it is paid for, to keep or take elsewhere. We keep only our own internal tools and templates.

We do not promise results

We do not guarantee a number of leads, calls, sales or a search ranking. Those depend on your market, your pricing and your follow-through. Any figure we discuss beforehand is an estimate.

Advertising spend is separate

Ad plans cover making the ads. What you spend to run them goes straight to the platform, at cost, never marked up by us. Same for any CRM, phone number or licence your setup needs — we tell you before a cost is incurred.

How you pay

By bank transfer against the invoice we email you. Monthly plans bill monthly in advance. Work starts once the first payment clears. We will never ask you for a card number, a banking password or a one-time code — if a message claims to be us and does, call (480) 798-0753 first.

Setup fees and paying yearly

Website subscriptions carry a one-off setup fee that pays for the build — charged once, never again. Paying a year ahead costs ten months for twelve, and still creates no lock-in: cancel and we refund the whole months you have not used.

If you stop a website subscription

You keep the site. Files handed over, hosting and domain moved into your name. Nothing is switched off or reverted — what stops is the ongoing work you were paying for.

Refunds

You cancel forward, not backward: a month in progress is not refunded. On one-time projects you get a full refund before we start, or a partial refund for work not yet done. Ad spend already placed cannot come back.

If something goes wrong

Our total liability is capped at the fees you paid us in the previous three months. Arizona law governs, Maricopa County courts, and we both agree to spend 30 days trying to sort it out directly before anyone files anything.

Typing your name here is a legally binding signature under the federal ESIGN Act and Arizona’s Electronic Transactions Act. We record the version you accepted and the date and time, and will send you that record on request.
