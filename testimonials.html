Search & Retrieval

STRATA

0.0066

average gap between its hand-written keyword search and the baselines published by other researchers, across five public datasets

A hybrid search engine — keyword, vector, fusion and re-ranking — written from scratch in numpy. The engine is not really the point; the measuring stick is. Landing that close to numbers other people published is how a stranger can tell the harness is not flattering itself. With a proper encoder in the vector leg, combining the two beats keyword search on all five datasets, every one at p ≤ 0.001.

Agent Evaluation

CRUCIBLE

27/30

tasks where friendly, obvious automation breaks a rule that actually matters

A simulated front desk for a small service company. An agent works one shift — quotes, bookings, refunds, and a few requests it ought to refuse — and ordinary Python checks what it actually did. No model grading another model. Every task ships its own answer key, and if the key cannot score 100% the task is treated as broken, not the agent.
