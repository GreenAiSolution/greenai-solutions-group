What Juno does, and what she will not.

Clear boundaries are part of the service. We agree the workflow, permissions and handoff rules before launch.

What Juno does

  • Handles calls and introduces herself as your AI receptionist
  • Books jobs only inside the prices, services and rules you approve
  • Call records and transcripts, configured around your consent requirements
  • Hands anything tricky to you instead of guessing

What she will not do

  • Pretend to be a human. Callers always know she is AI.
  • Quote prices or promise timelines you did not approve.
  • Handle emergencies. She tells the caller to hang up and dial 911.
  • Replace your judgment on big, unusual or sensitive jobs. Those go to you.
