Meet the Founder

Hi, I'm Jaden Green.

I'm Jaden Green. I hold a B.S. in Real Estate and Business from the University of Washington, and I've spent seven years building systems that let a small company run like a much bigger one. GreenAI is where all of that work now lives.

The degree matters more than it sounds like it should. I learned to read a P&L, a market and a margin before I ever automated anything — so what I sell you isn't technology, it's a job that gets done faster, cheaper, or at an hour when nobody is awake. Every build starts with the question an owner actually asks: what does this return, and when?

I build the systems myself — not licensed off someone else's platform, not handed to a contractor overseas. Seven years of doing it that way produced one rule I don't break: whoever does the work doesn't grade it. A second system checks the first, a person approves anything that acts on your behalf, and the machine is built to say “I'm not sure” instead of inventing an answer. That is the difference between AI you can put in front of your customers and AI you have to apologise for.

What that looks like on your side of the desk: every call, form and text answered in seconds and held until somebody owns it. Follow-up that actually follows up. Ads rewritten the moment they tire. Books, dispatch and records that close themselves. You stay the owner — you just stop being the bottleneck. And you work with me directly, every time.

Our Mission

Why We Do This

GreenAI exists to make enterprise-grade AI accessible to the businesses that were never meant to afford it — the two-truck contractor, the single-chair salon, the office of three. The technology is here. We make it work for you.

Level the Playing Field

A two-truck operation deserves the same tools as the company with a call centre. AI is what closes that gap — and we make sure our clients are the ones holding it.

Eliminate Wasted Time

Answering the phone, chasing quotes, retyping the same details into three places — the work that fills your evenings without paying for them. We hand that back so you can spend the hours on the job and the people you serve.

Real Partnership

We don't sell software and disappear. Every client gets a genuine strategic partner committed to your results. Your wins are our wins — that's not a marketing line, it's how we operate.

The Opportunity

Why This Matters
For a Local Business

The last big shift for local business was the internet. Getting found moved from the phone book to Google, and the businesses that adapted got the calls. The ones that waited competed for what was left.

AI is that shift again, but it lands somewhere different. It doesn't change how customers find you — it changes what happens in the minutes after they do. Who answers. How fast. Whether anyone follows up on Saturday at 8pm.

For a business owner running the job and the phone at the same time, that gap is where the money leaks out. Not in marketing spend — in the calls that rang out, the quotes never sent, the leads that went cold while you were on a roof.

That gap is the whole reason this company exists. Close it, and the marketing you already pay for starts working harder.

24/7

Nights, weekends, and the middle of a job. An AI employee picks up, gets the details, and books the work while your competitor's phone rings out.

Minutes

Most people calling a service business call three. The one who replies first usually gets the job — that's the whole race, and it is winnable with software.
