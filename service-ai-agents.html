What it does.

One agent, trained on your prices, hours, service area and wording. It works where your leads already arrive and books where you already book.

Replies in under a minute

Website form, Facebook and Instagram messages, Google Business messages, Gmail and text. Every new lead gets a friendly, specific answer, day or night.

Answers from your price list

The common questions, in your words and your prices. A price that is not on your list cannot go out. When it does not know, it says so and hands it to you.

Books the appointment

Offers two real times and books straight onto your calendar, Jobber, Housecall Pro or ServiceTitan, with a confirmation to the customer and a note to you.

Follows up until they answer

Day one, day three, day seven. Short, polite, in your voice. Then it stops. Most booked jobs come from the second or third message.

Hands you the unusual ones

A commercial job, an upset customer, a question outside your rules: it drafts a reply and waits for your yes instead of guessing.

Shows you the count

A Monday note: leads in, replied in how long, booked, still being followed up, handed to you. Matched against your own calendar.

What you will not get.

  • Not a person, and never pretends to be

    If a customer asks, it says it is the company's automated assistant and who will follow up. That is a rule in every script.

  • Built for written leads

    Website forms, Facebook, Google, Gmail and text. If you need the phone line itself answered, say so in the first conversation and you get a straight answer about what is possible.

  • No invented prices or promises

    A number that is not on your list is blocked before it sends. Anything outside your rules comes to you as a draft, not to the customer.

  • No promise of more jobs

    How many leads become customers depends on your prices, your area and the season. What is promised is the reply and the follow-up happening every time, the way you approved.

Asked before starting

Where does it work?

Your website form, Facebook and Instagram messages, Google Business Profile messages, Gmail and text. Jobber, Housecall Pro, ServiceTitan or a plain Google Calendar for booking. If you use something else, ask.

Do my customers know it is an AI?

If they ask, it tells the truth. It does not pretend to be a person and does not use a fake name.

What happens when it does not know the answer?

It says so, takes the details, and the conversation lands in your inbox with a drafted reply waiting for your yes. It never guesses a price.

I already use Jobber or Housecall Pro. Does this replace it?

No, it feeds it. The client, the request and the booking land in the tool you already run.

How long until it is live?

Usually inside two weeks from the day you answer the setup questions, and you approve every reply before it goes live.
