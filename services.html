Ad fatigue is a clock, not a maybe

Run one ad and it works until the same people have seen it four times. Then your cost per customer climbs every single week, and the platform quietly charges you more to reach the same audience. The only real defence is a fresh angle before the last one tires out.

That is a production problem, and production is exactly what got cheap. We write, generate and cut a steady stream of new ads for you every month — different hooks, different formats, different lengths — so there is always another one ready when the current one flattens out.
