A long snapper has one job. Put the ball in the same place, at the same speed, every time, with someone running at you. Nobody knows your name unless you miss.

I did that for five years at Washington: a Pac-12 championship, a Sugar Bowl, a national championship game. What it taught me was not football. It was how to be automatic when it matters.

That is what I build now. RING answers the phone the same way at 9:47 on a Sunday night as it does on Monday morning. Nobody notices it. That is the point.

How I got here.

  1. Age 17
    Started building

    I started at seventeen and never stopped. Seven years on, it is systems that let a small company run like a much bigger one.

  2. 2020 – 2025
    University of Washington

    Five years on a full football scholarship in Seattle. 5 a.m. lifts, film graded every week, performance judged in public. I finished a degree in art with a minor in real estate, which is why the sites look the way they do and why I read the numbers before I automate anything.

  3. Early 2025
    The ramp at Sky Harbor

    Ground crew on aircraft turns. A clock that does not move and a checklist that does not bend. I started GreenAI the same winter.

  4. 2025 – 2026
    A service route, and a lot of doors

    A year inside a small Phoenix service company: a weekly route of homes, and door-to-door sales through a full Arizona summer. I saw from the inside what a small company's day looks like, and how much of it is the phone, the follow-up and the invoice.

  5. Now
    GreenAI Solutions

    Six AI employees that work inside the apps a company already uses, websites coded by hand, finished ads and custom systems. We are a team of 12 now, and my number is still the one on the site.

Three rules I don't break.

Whoever does the work does not grade it

The system does the job, a second system checks it, and a person approves it before it acts. A price that is not on your list gets blocked, not sent.

"I'm not sure" beats a good guess

Everything I build is made to say so and hand it to you, rather than invent an answer in front of your customer.

You can read it all first

The prices, the service agreement and the work are public. Month to month, and everything built is yours if you leave.
