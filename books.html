The job

What BOOKS does inside QuickBooks Online

Six things, all of them in the tool you already open every day.

Sends the invoice when the job closes

Built from your QuickBooks items and customers, with your payment link, the same day.

Reminds late payers politely

Day seven, fourteen and twenty-one, in your tone. Never a threat, never a surprise fee.

Matches payments and marks them paid

Card, bank and check payments reconciled against the open invoice.

A cash sheet every Monday

What is owed, what is late, what came in last week. One page.

Flags disputes to you

A customer who questions a charge gets a human, not a reminder.

Stops when you say stop

Pause reminders for one customer or all of them in one message.

Questions

Asked before hiring BOOKS

  • Will it annoy my customers?

    Three reminders over three weeks, written by you or approved by you. Most owners find it gentler than what they were doing.

  • Does it touch my books?

    It creates invoices, records payments and marks them paid, through QuickBooks itself. Your accountant sees ordinary QuickBooks entries.
