Everything a Phoenix service business asks before handing its phone to an AI — what it costs, what it does when it does not know something, and how fast you can walk away. Answered straight, including the ones where the answer is no.

How it works

06 QUESTIONS
What is an “AI employee”, in plain English?

It is software that picks up your phone, answers your texts and replies to your web-form leads — in your business’s own words. Not a chat bubble on your website, and not a person. It answers in about eight seconds, works out what the caller needs, and puts the appointment straight into your calendar. One employee doing four jobs: taking the calls that come in, making the follow-up calls you never get around to, answering the front-desk questions, and confirming tomorrow’s schedule.

Do my customers know they are talking to an AI?

If they ask, yes. It does not pretend to be a person and it will not take a human name. Ask it straight out whether it is a real person and it tells you the truth. You can try that yourself on the live demo — it is one of the things we suggest you try.

Do I keep my own phone number?

Yes. Your number stays yours and nothing on your truck, your cards or your Google listing has to change. At setup you choose how it works: either the AI answers first, or it picks up only the calls you miss.

What happens when it does not know the answer?

It says so. That is deliberate — the system is built to say “I’m not sure” rather than invent an answer, because an invented answer is the one thing you would have to apologise for. When that happens it takes the details, tells the caller you will follow up, and the whole conversation lands in your inbox so you can.

What actually happens at 2am?

The same thing that happens at 2pm. Every call, text and web form is answered in about eight seconds — nights, weekends, holidays, monsoon season. It books what it can book and sends you the full transcript. You read it over coffee instead of losing the lead.

Does this replace my receptionist?

No, and it is not sold as one. It covers the hours and the calls nobody is covering — the phone that rings out at 7pm, the missed call you meant to return on Tuesday, the second caller while your person is already on the line. If you have someone at the desk, this is what happens when they cannot get to it.

What it costs

06 QUESTIONS
What does $497 a month cover?

One AI employee and unlimited leads. Answering every call, text and web form; booking into your calendar; chasing the ones who go quiet; full transcripts in your inbox; and me maintaining it, including changing what it says whenever you want it changed. Flat rate — a busy month costs exactly the same as a quiet one.

Is there a setup fee or a per-call charge?

No to both. No setup fee, no per-call charge, no per-minute charge, no charge for a busy month. $497 is the whole price. That is the part we think is broken about metered plans: they charge you more for having a good month.

What is not included?

Anything paid to someone else, which we never mark up: a phone number if you need a new one, any CRM or scheduling tool you want it to write into, and advertising spend if you also run ads with us. We tell you about a required third-party cost before it is incurred, not after.

How do I pay, and when am I charged?

By card or bank debit through Stripe, our payment processor. Your details go on Stripe’s own checkout page — never on this website and never to me directly. The first payment is taken when you place the order, and it renews on the same day each month, in advance. Prefer a bank transfer and an invoice? Say so and we do it that way.

Can I cancel?

Any time. Month to month, no minimum term, no lock-in and no cancellation fee. Email jaden@greenaidigital.com or call (480) 798-0753. It stops at the end of the month you have already paid for — you cancel forward, not backward — and we do not bill you again after that.

Do you guarantee I will get more bookings?

No, and be wary of anyone who does. How many leads turn into jobs depends on your market, your pricing, your offer and your follow-through, and none of those are mine to control. Any number we discuss in advance is an estimate, not a promise. What I do stand behind is the system doing what I said it does.

Getting it running

04 QUESTIONS
How long until it is live?

About two weeks from the day you answer the setup questions. There are four steps: you tell us how you answer the phone, we build it, you test it yourself, and then it goes on duty.

What do you need from me?

One round of questions — your trade, your hours, your service area, your prices, and the questions you always ask a new customer — plus access to whatever it needs to book into, usually your calendar. Email or a call, your choice. If we end up waiting on you, the go-live date moves by however long we wait.

Can I hear it before my customers do?

Yes, and you should try to break it. Nothing goes live until you have called your own number and tried to trip it up on your own questions. If you want to hear the idea right now, before any of this, the demo on this site is a working phone call in your browser.

What if I want it to say something differently?

You tell me and I change it. That is included in the monthly — no change fee, no ticket queue, no waiting for a release. It is the reason the price is flat.

Risk, control and who you are dealing with

06 QUESTIONS
What if it books a job I cannot do?

You set the boundaries and it works inside them: only the slots you actually have open, only the services you told it you offer, only inside your service area. Anyone outside that gets their details taken and handed to you as a lead — it does not commit you to work you did not agree to.

Who is responsible if it says something wrong?

Straight answer: AI output can be wrong. That is why a second system checks the first, why it is built to say “I’m not sure” instead of guessing, and why you approve what it says before it goes live. The agreement splits it plainly — you are responsible for how your business runs, and I am responsible for the system doing what I said it would do. If it says something you do not like, tell me and it changes that day.

What happens to my customers’ information?

It stays yours. You own your data, your configuration and your transcripts, and both of us keep the other’s non-public business information confidential — during the work and after it ends. If you leave, you take all of it with you.

I already use Jobber or Housecall Pro. Does this replace it?

No — it feeds it. Bookings land in your calendar. If your scheduling lives in Jobber, Housecall Pro or ServiceTitan, tell me during setup and we work out how bookings get there. Where a direct connection is not possible on your plan, it books into your calendar and emails you the full details so nothing is retyped twice.

Do you only work with Phoenix businesses?

It is designed around Phoenix service businesses — that is the market it was built for, and it is where I am. But nothing about it is physically local. If you are outside the Valley and it sounds like it fits, email me and say so.

Who am I actually dealing with?

Me — Jaden Green, in Gilbert, Arizona. GreenAI Solutions is owner-operated: I build the system, I maintain it, and you deal with me directly rather than a rep, a reseller or a support queue. (480) 798-0753.
