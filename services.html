Review automation.

When someone is choosing who to call, they look at the stars and the number next to them. After each job or visit, your customer automatically gets a short, personal text asking for a review, with one friendly reminder if they forget. It is the always-on version of the review cards, and every new review gets a reply drafted in your voice for you to approve. Everyone is asked the same way, because Google and the FTC forbid sorting the happy from the unhappy first.
