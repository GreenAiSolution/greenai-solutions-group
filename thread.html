The job

What THREAD does inside Slack

Six things, all of them in the tool you already open every day.

Answers the team from your own documents

"What did we quote the Hendersons?" "Are we open Saturday?" Answered in the thread, with the source.

Posts every lead, call and payment as it happens

One channel becomes the live feed of the business. Nothing gets lost in a text to one person.

Drafts the customer reply in the thread

A teammate reacts with one emoji to send it, or edits it first. Nobody types from scratch.

A morning summary in the channel you choose

What came in overnight, what got handled, what needs a person.

Takes questions in a direct message

Ask it privately what a job is worth or where a file is. Same answers, no noise in the channel.

Says so when it does not know

It will not guess at a price or a policy. It says it is not sure and tags the person who knows.

How it connects

Your account. Your rules.

  • A private app in your own workspace

    Installed by you, owned by you. Not a marketplace bot with a thousand other companies on it.

  • Only the channels you invite it to

    Slack's own permissions, listed in writing. Remove it from a channel or the workspace in one click.

  • Your documents, your rules

    Price list, hours, policies and job notes you choose. It reads nothing you did not hand it.
