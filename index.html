AI ads · Frame by frame

How an ad gets made.

No black box. Here is the path from a rough idea to a finished ad, and eight frames from one of our films.

Brief

One offer, one promise, one customer. We write it down before anyone generates a frame.

Boards

We sketch the first three seconds and the end card. The hook is decided here, not later.

Generate

Leading video models, many takes per shot. We keep what feels cinematic and throw away the rest.

Direct

Fix drift, extend a shot, swap a take. Every frame is reviewed by a person.

Grade and cut

Color, pacing, captions and sound. Your logo and text go on in editing so they stay exact.

Deliver

Reel, feed and wide versions, ready to upload to Meta, TikTok, YouTube or your site.

Straight answers

Before you ask.

What can you automate?

Most of the repeat work between a customer reaching out and you getting paid: replies to leads, missed calls, quote follow-up, booking and reminders, review asks, invoices and payment reminders, inbox sorting and daily summaries. The free map tells you which ones are worth it for you.

Do I have to switch software?

No. We build inside the apps you already use, like your email, calendar, phone, website forms and accounting. If something you use cannot connect, we tell you before you pay.

Will it sound like a robot to my customers?

Messages are written in your voice and you approve them before anything goes live. Anything unusual gets passed to you or your team instead of guessed at.

How much does automation cost?

The map is free. Plans start at $497 to build one automation and $197 a month to run it. Front Office is $1,497 to build and $497 a month, and Autopilot is $2,997 and $997 a month. The build price is fixed in writing before any work starts.

Is that real footage in the ads?

No. Every film on this site is made with AI. They are concept films, labeled as such. Your ads are built from your business details and your own photos and footage where you have them.

What does AI do badly?

Hands, small text and logos can drift. We review every frame and add your logo and text ourselves in editing so they stay exact. If a shot does not hold up, we do not use it.

Will this get me more leads?

We make strong ads and set up fast replies. Where you run them and how much you spend is your call, so we do not promise a number of leads. We do promise you see your own ad before paying.

What do the free map and free sample include?

The map is a plain list of the three automations worth doing first for your business. The sample is one ad made for your business so you can judge the work. No card and no obligation for either.

Who is behind Clipcode?

Jaden Green, in Gilbert, Arizona. Clipcode used to be called GreenAI Digital; same team, same email, new name. You deal with a real person you can email or call.

Do you only work in Phoenix?

Phoenix businesses come first, but the work is done online, so businesses elsewhere can send a request.
