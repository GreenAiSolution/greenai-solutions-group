    Process

    How a build runs

    The same four steps whether it is a five-page site or a phone line.

    1. One conversation

      What is stuck, what it costs you now, and what done would look like. About twenty minutes, by phone or email.

    2. A price in writing

      What gets built, what it will not do, and the number. Nothing is signed until you have read it.

    3. Built in the open

      You see it working on real examples as it comes together. Every script, page and ad goes past you first.

    4. Live, and still answered

      Changes are same-day at no charge. The number on this page rings the person who built it.
