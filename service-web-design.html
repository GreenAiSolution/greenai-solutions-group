What It Actually Is

A site designed to turn a visitor into an enquiry.

A website is not a brochure. It has one job: someone lands on it, understands within about five seconds what you do and whether it is for them, and then has an obvious next step. Everything else on the page is decoration.

So the build starts with what you sell and who buys it, not with picking a theme. The layout follows the argument — what they need to believe, in what order, before they will call you.

It is built mobile-first, because most of your traffic is a phone held in one hand, often on a bad connection. If it is fast and clear there, it will be fine everywhere else.

The SEO foundation goes in during the build, not as an afterthought: proper structure, real page titles and descriptions, indexing configured so Google can actually read it. That is groundwork, not a ranking promise.

You own it. Permanently.

The files are yours the day it launches. No platform lock-in, no monthly fee just to keep your own site online, and no hostage situation if you ever want to work with someone else.

Maintenance is optional and separate at $150/mo. If you do not want it, the site still runs.

Questions

The things people ask before they buy.

Seven business days — really?

From the brief being done, yes, for Starter and Business. The clock is honest about what it measures: it starts when we have what we need from you, and the review day is yours.

What do I actually need to give you?

Your services and prices, any photos or logos you have, and an hour for the brief. If you have no photos, we work around it — that is normal.

Do you write the words?

For a short site, with you, in the brief. A large content-heavy site is scoped separately so nobody pretends a ten-page rewrite was included.

What if I want changes after launch?

Small things right after launch are part of the handover. Ongoing changes are what the $150/mo maintenance is for — and it is genuinely optional.

Who owns the site?

You do, completely, from launch. Domain and hosting in your name, files handed to you. Walk away whenever you like and take it with you.

Can you redo a site I already have?

Yes, and that is most of this work. Same packages, priced by the size of the finished site rather than by what it used to be.
