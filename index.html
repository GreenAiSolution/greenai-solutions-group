Asked before hiring

All the questions

What is the AI agent, in plain English?

Software that replies to every new lead in your company's own words, from your website form, Facebook, Google, Gmail or text. It answers from your price list, books the appointment and follows up until they answer. Not a chat bubble, and not a person.

What does review automation do?

After every finished job your customer gets a short text asking for a review, one reminder if they forget, and every new review gets a reply drafted in your voice for you to approve.

Do my customers know they are talking to an AI?

If they ask, it tells the truth. It does not pretend to be a person and does not use a fake name.

Is there a setup fee or a per-lead charge?

No. $397 a month for the agent, $149 a month for review automation, and that is the whole price.

Can I cancel?

At the end of any month you have paid for. You keep every script, transcript and file. Nothing stops working when you leave.
