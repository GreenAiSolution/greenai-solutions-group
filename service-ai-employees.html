What It Actually Is

Not a phone tree. A member of staff who happens to be software.

An AI employee is a voice that answers your business line. It knows your hours, your prices, your service area and how you talk about what you do, because we sit down with you and train it on exactly that before it takes a single call.

A caller doesn't press 1 for anything. They say what they want. It answers, asks the questions a good receptionist would ask, and then does something with the answer — books the job, texts a confirmation, or hands the call to you.

Four of them exist, and you pick the ones your business actually needs. STERLING handles inbound sales — picks up first ring, qualifies, books. HUNTER makes the calls you never get around to: every missed call rung back, every lead that went cold months ago. MAVEN runs the front desk — hours, pricing, directions, "do you service my area", answered properly every time. LEDGER handles scheduling and ops: confirms tomorrow's jobs, chases the no-shows, and refills the slot before it dies.

You do not build it, host it, prompt it, or babysit it. We build it, we maintain it, and if it needs to change because your prices changed, you send one message and we change it.

The flat-rate difference

Everywhere else in this category charges per call or per minute. That means a good month — the one where the phone rings off the hook — costs you the most money.

We think that's backwards. One flat fee, unlimited calls. A busy month costs exactly the same as a quiet one.

How It Works

From signing up to it running.

We learn your business

A working session where we go through your services, your prices, your hours, your service area, and the ten questions customers actually ask. This is the part that makes it sound like your business instead of a robot.

Day 1

We write the fence before the prompt

Every agent gets six lines in writing before a word of prompt is written: what it CAN do, what it must REFUSE, its hard CAP, the HOURS it may act, the exact trigger that ESCALATES to a human, and how you UNDO anything it did. You get that document. If a vendor can't hand you one, the fence doesn't exist.

Day 1–2

We build and connect it

The number is set up, the calendar is connected, the text-back is wired, and it is trained on everything from step one. You get a live number to call yourself and try to break.

Day 2–5

You test it before anyone else does

You call it. Your spouse calls it. Your least patient employee calls it. Anything that sounds wrong gets changed before a real customer ever reaches it.

Before launch

It goes live and we keep it running

Your line points at it. From then on it answers everything. When something needs to change, you message us and we change it — that's included, not an extra.

Straight Answers

Where an AI employee stops — and where it loses.

Every one of these is written into the fence document before it takes a call, so nothing here is a surprise later.

  • ✕It will not quote a custom job. Anything that needs eyes on the work goes to you, with the caller's details captured.
  • ✕It will not discount, negotiate, or promise an arrival window unless you explicitly configure one.
  • ✕It will not take card details over the phone. Payment links get texted instead.
  • ✕It hands over the moment someone is upset. Complaint, refund, "let me speak to a person" — three strikes or one trigger word, and it escalates.
  • ✕Under roughly 40 calls a month, a metered plan is cheaper than $497. That is the honest math and we will tell you so on the call. Past that, metering charges you more for having a good month, which is the part we think is broken.

Questions

The things people ask before they buy.

How long until it is actually answering my phone?

Usually inside a week from the first working session. Most of that time is us learning your business properly; the build itself is fast. Nothing goes live until you have called it yourself and been happy with what you heard.

Do I have to change my phone number?

No. Your existing number forwards to it. Customers dial the same number they always have.

What happens if it does not know an answer?

It says it does not know and offers to have a human call back — then captures the details and notifies you. It is built to never invent an answer, because a confident wrong answer costs you a customer.

Can I listen to what it said on a call?

Yes. You get transcripts. You can read exactly what was said rather than take our word for it.

What if my prices change?

Message us and we update it. Changes are included in the monthly rate — that is the difference between hiring this and buying a toolkit you have to maintain yourself.

Can I cancel?

Any time. No contract, no cancellation fee, no notice period. If it is not earning its keep, it should not stay.
