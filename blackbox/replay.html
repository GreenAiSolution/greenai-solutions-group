What you are looking at

Every record is chained to the one before it: its hash is SHA-256 of the previous hash plus the record itself. Edit one field, drop one line, or slip one in, and every hash after it stops matching. The badge at the top is not a claim; your browser just recomputed all of them. Press tamper to change one record in memory and watch the chain break at that exact point.

Across is the tool that was called, up is time, depth is the run. A run that went the way it should is a clean violet-and-amber climb. A red point is a policy block or an error, with the reason in the record.
