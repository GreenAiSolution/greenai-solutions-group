What It Actually Is

Volume is the strategy, because you cannot pick the winner in advance.

Every month you get a batch of finished, ready-to-run ads for your business. Not concepts, not templates with your logo dropped in — finished files you could upload this afternoon.

They are not twenty-five versions of the same ad. They are a spread: different hooks (the problem, the question, the before-and-after), different styles (testimonial, founder-to-camera, offer-led, urgency), and different cuts (15-second vertical, 30-second story, square feed) built from the same idea.

That spread is the entire point. Nobody — not us, not an agency charging you $200 an ad — can tell you in advance which angle your market responds to. You can only find out, and you find out faster with twenty-five shots than with one.

The scripts and hooks get written first, before anything is visual. The angle is the work. The visuals follow it.

What 25 ads costs, three ways

From an agency: around $5,000, at roughly $200 each — and that is before usage rights.

From a DIY tool: $110–$249/mo, plus the twenty-odd hours a month you spend actually making them.

From us: $1,297, about $52 each, finished and delivered. Your hours stay yours.

How It Works

From signing up to it running.

We take the brief once

What you sell, who buys it, what your offer is, and what has worked or flopped before. This happens once, not every month.

Week 1

Scripts and hooks first

Before anything is filmed or rendered, the angles get written out. You see the hooks. Anything that does not sound like your business gets cut at this stage, when changing it is free.

Week 1

The batch gets produced

Every approved angle is built out into its formats — vertical, square, 15s, 30s — with captions burned in, because most of the feed plays on mute.

Week 1–2

You get the files

Delivered as finished files you own outright. Run them on Meta, TikTok, YouTube, anywhere, forever. No usage window, no licence to renew.

Every month

The next batch lands before fatigue does

A new set arrives before the last one tires. That cadence is the product — ad fatigue never gets a chance to bite.

Questions

The things people ask before they buy.

When does the first batch arrive?

Inside the first two weeks. The brief and the script approval take up most of that; once the angles are signed off, production is quick.

Do I really own the files?

Yes, outright and permanently. Run them on any platform, for as long as you like, including after you cancel. There is no usage window and no licence to renew.

What if I hate the hooks?

You see them before anything is produced, which is exactly why that step exists. Cut the ones that do not sound like you and we write more.

Can you run the ads too?

Ad management is a separate service, not part of this package. Ask and we will tell you honestly whether you need it yet.

Do I need to be on camera?

No. Founder-to-camera is one style among several. If you would rather not be in them, the batch is built from the other angles.

Can I switch tiers?

Any month. Move up when spend justifies it, move down when it does not.
