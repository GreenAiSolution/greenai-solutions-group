Straight talk

What Juno does, and what she will not.

Clear boundaries are part of the service. We agree the workflow, permissions and handoff rules before launch.

What Juno does

  • Handles calls and introduces herself as your AI receptionist
  • Books jobs only inside the prices, services and rules you approve
  • Call records and transcripts, configured around your consent requirements
  • Hands anything tricky to you instead of guessing

What she will not do

  • Pretend to be a human. Callers always know she is AI.
  • Quote prices or promise timelines you did not approve.
  • Handle emergencies. She tells the caller to hang up and dial 911.
  • Replace your judgment on big, unusual or sensitive jobs. Those go to you.

Questions

Asked before hiring.

All the questions

Do I need to change how I work?+

No. The AI employees live inside your phone, Jobber, Gmail, Slack, Teams and QuickBooks. You keep working the way you work now.

What if the AI gets something wrong?+

Nothing goes to a customer without your rules. You set the boundaries and approve the playbook before anything goes live. Every call is recorded and transcribed, so you can check the work.

Are there contracts?+

No. Month to month, cancel at the end of any month you have paid for.

Who owns the ads?+

You do. Every file delivered is yours, forever, whether you stay or go.

How fast do we launch?+

Most businesses are live within 7 days of the setup call.

Do you work with my trade?+

If you serve local customers and take calls, yes. Roofing, HVAC, plumbing, pools, landscaping and more.

Which employee should I start with? +

Start with Juno, the phone line. She answers the calls you are missing right now, and she usually pays for herself in the first month.

Can I add more employees later? +

Yes. Upgrade anytime and the new employee is live within days. You only ever pay for what you run.

What does setup involve? +

One call. We connect to your phone, calendar and tools, learn your prices and wording, and you approve everything before it goes live.

Do I need to film anything?+

No. The ads are made with AI creative plus stock and generated footage, directed for your trade. If you have job-site clips you love, we will happily use them, but nothing is required.

Who owns the files?+

You do. Every finished ad is yours forever, whether you stay or go.

Can I cancel?+

Anytime. Month to month, monthly plans, and you keep every ad we ever delivered.

What does setup cost?+

One-time setup is $497 per service, and it is done for you. Bundle AI Employees with Creative AI Ads and setup is free.

How does the bundle discount work?+

Run both services and you save 15% off the combined monthly price, every month, for as long as you run both.

What if it does not work for us?+

Then you cancel. That is the point of month to month. And everything we built for you, every script, transcript and file, stays yours.
