Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to be tech-savvy to use your services?

Absolutely not. We handle all the technical setup, integration, and ongoing management. You'll use simple, intuitive tools — most of which work inside your existing CRM or email platform. If you can send a text message, you can use our AI tools.

How quickly does it go live?

An AI employee is answering within about a week — we spend the first few days learning your services, pricing and the questions you actually get asked. Your first ad batch lands inside two weeks. From day one you get a log of every call and message it handled, so you can read exactly what it said and what it booked.

Will the AI sound like me?

Yes — this is a priority for us. During onboarding we collect writing samples, learn your tone, and train the AI to communicate in your voice. You review and approve every flow before it goes live, and you can change the wording any time.

What CRMs and tools do you integrate with?

Tell us what you already use — your CRM, calendar, inbox, phone system or booking tool — and we will say plainly what can be connected before you buy.

Is there a long-term contract?

No. All GreenAI plans are month-to-month. We earn your business every month through results, not contracts. Cancel anytime — no questions asked.

Do you work with agents outside of Arizona?

Yes — while we're based in Gilbert, AZ, the setup can be handled remotely when the business is a fit. Tell us where you operate and we will confirm what makes sense before you buy.
