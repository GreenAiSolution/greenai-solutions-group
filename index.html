The rules they work inside

What they will not do.

NET

  • Never invents a price. If the answer is not on your list, it says the owner will confirm.
  • Never claims to be a person.
  • Does not promise a booking count. It promises the reply and the follow-up.

BALANCE

  • Does not diagnose equipment or give chemical dosing over text — it books a tech visit.
  • Does not send anything a customer has not agreed to receive; one “stop” ends all messages.
  • Never posts a review or writes one — it only asks the customer to.

PUMP

  • Never changes a price, offers a discount, or waives a fee on its own.
  • Never threatens, mentions collections agencies or credit, or contacts anyone but the account holder.
  • Does not process payments itself — it sends the link to the processor you already use.
