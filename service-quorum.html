How the work moves

Six steps. Nobody marks their own homework.

This is the part you are actually paying for. Anyone can put a prompt into three chatbots. The value is in the order they go in, what each one is shown, and what is deliberately hidden from them.

1

The brief

One call, about forty-five minutes. We write down what you sell, who buys it, what you charge, what you have already tried, and what “this worked” would look like ninety days from now. Every model gets this same document and nothing else.

You + Jaden
2

Three passes, in parallel, sealed off from each other

Gemini reads the market. GPT swings at the offer and the creative. Claude builds the working version. None of the three sees what the others produced. You end up with three genuinely different attempts at your business — not one attempt with two rubber stamps on it.

Gemini · research GPT · creative Claude · build
3

Blind cross-review

Now they read each other — with the names stripped off, so no model knows whose work it is marking. Three models, two reviews each: six reviews on every deliverable. Anything two of the three independently flag comes out. Anything only one flags gets argued.

reviews O + A reviews G + A reviews G + O
4

The merge

The strongest version becomes the spine, and the best single ideas from the other two get grafted onto it. You get a one-page note showing which line came from which seat and why it survived. No black box, no “the AI said so”.

Claude assembles
5

The fact gate — a person, every time

Jaden reads the whole thing before you do. Every number, name, credential and promise has to trace back to something real or it does not ship. Nothing about price, contracts or commitments is ever written by an AI and sent without a human reading it first. This gate has no automatic override.

6

Delivered, and it is yours

Files, source code, ad assets, accounts and logins — handed over. No platform you have to stay on, no licence that expires, nothing that stops working if you walk away. Same ownership promise as everything else we sell.

Where this stops, and where we lose

If any of the following is a dealbreaker, we would rather you found out now than in week two.

Three models will not save a bad offer.

If what you sell does not sell, this makes that obvious faster — it does not fix it. Sometimes the honest answer at step 4 is “change the thing you are selling”. We will say it.

No AI cold-calling. Not now, not for extra.

The FCC treats an AI-generated voice as “artificial” under the TCPA, so cold calls made with one need written consent first — and most small-business numbers are cell phones. Your AI employee answers calls that come in and handles replies. It does not dial strangers.

Ten business days is the floor, not a rush option.

Three sealed passes and six reviews take the time they take. If you need something live this week, buy the AI employee on its own at $497/month and we will start there.

Ad spend is yours, and it is not included.

Fair questions

Before you apply

Could I not just ask ChatGPT this myself?

You could, and for some of it you should. What you cannot easily do alone is run the same brief through three competing labs, keep them from seeing each other, get them to mark each other’s work blind, and then actually build and ship the result. That process — and the person who refuses to publish anything that does not check out — is the product.

Why does having three matter? Aren’t they all much the same?

They are not. They were trained by different companies on different material and they fail in different ways. A number one model invents confidently is often the first thing another one questions. Using three is the cheapest reliable way to catch that before it reaches your customers.

Who owns what comes out of it?

You do — the site, the code, the images, the copy, the accounts. In writing, in the Service Agreement, same as every other thing we sell. Stopping payment does not switch anything off or take anything back.

Do you use my business information to train these models?

No. Your material is used to do your work. We do not sell it, publish it, or hand it to anyone as training data, and we will name every tool your material passes through if you ask.

What if I do not like the direction?

Step 2 gives you three different directions on purpose, and you choose at step 4 — before anything gets built. That is the point of the sealed passes: the choice is genuinely yours, and it happens early enough to be cheap.

Why “by application”?

Because one of these runs at a time and it takes ten days. If your business is not a fit — wrong stage, wrong problem, or you simply do not need this much — we will say so on the call and point you at the $497 option instead.
